Frequently Asked Questions

About SailPro

What is SailPro?

A year-round sailing training base in Larnaca, Cyprus. We offer professional coaching, top-quality charter boats and tailored programs for sailors from around the world, from beginners to elite competitors.

Where are you based?

Larnaca Nautical Club, Larnaca-Dhekelia Road, 6308, Larnaca, Cyprus.

Which boat classes do you train?

Optimist and ILCA.

Do you run all year?

Yes. SailPro is a year-round training base.

What are the weather and wind like?

The Mediterranean climate is mild all year. The coldest month is February, with an average daily temperature of 12°C. In winter the wind can come from any direction and at any strength, giving sailors the opportunity to train at different conditions. In summer there’s a steady sea breeze from the south-southwest, building through the day and averaging around 16 knots.

Clinics

What are your clinics?

Training camps for Optimist and ILCA sailors led by experienced coaches from around the world. They are designed to improve racing performance, technique and understanding of the sport, and some focus on specific areas such as boat handling and speed, race strategy or mindset.

Winter training, regattas and lessons

What does the winter training base include?

Charter boats, coach RIBs, secure boat storage and a dedicated classroom for debriefs and theory sessions.

Can sailors join individually, or must they bring a team?

Both. Sailors can join individually and train with the Larnaca Nautical Club team under its coach, or come with their own coach and teammates.

What do your regatta services cover?

Complete arrangements for regattas across Cyprus: event registration, charter boats, coach or support RIBs and logistics.

Do you offer private lessons?

Yes, for complete beginners and experienced sailors who want one-to-one instruction. Lessons are tailored to your level and needs.

Booking and practical information

How do I book or get a quote?

Email sailpro.cy@gmail.com, WhatsApp +357 96774178, or use the contact form.

How much does it cost?

We provide quotes only. Contact us by email or WhatsApp and we will send you a quote.

What do sailors need to bring?

Sailors bring their own sails, control lines, tiller and tiller extension.

Can you help with accommodation?

Yes, we can assist you in finding accommodation if needed.

How do I get to SailPro?

The closest airport is Larnaca International Airport, a 20-minute drive from SailPro. You can take a bus or a taxi, or we can help arrange transportation.
