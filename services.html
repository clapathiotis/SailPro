Our Services

At SailPro, we provide a complete range of services for sailors and teams training or competing in Cyprus. From international coaching clinics and winter training programs to regatta support and charter options, our goal is to make your sailing experience smooth, professional, and rewarding. Whether you’re here to train, race, or both, SailPro offers everything you need — all in one place, in the heart of the Mediterranean.

Clinics

At SailPro, we host high-quality sailing clinics for the Optimist and ILCA classes, led by experienced coaches from around the world. Each camp is designed to help sailors improve their racing performance, technique, and overall understanding of the sport. Some clinics are specialized, focusing on specific areas based on each coach’s expertise — from boat handling and speed to race strategy and mindset. Join our training sessions and develop your skills in the perfect Mediterranean sailing conditions of Larnaca, Cyprus

Winter Training Base

SailPro offers the ideal winter training base for sailors and teams looking to escape the cold. With mild Mediterranean weather and reliable sailing conditions throughout the season, Larnaca provides a great setting for consistent training. Our facilities include charter boats, ribs, a boat park, and space for theory sessions — everything you need for productive and enjoyable winter practice.

Regatta Services

SailPro organizes complete arrangements for sailors wishing to compete in regattas across Cyprus. We handle everything from event registration and charter boats to coach or support ribs, logistics, and on-site assistance. Our goal is to make participation easy and stress-free, so you can focus entirely on your performance on the water.
