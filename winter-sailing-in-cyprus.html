Winter Sailing in Cyprus

Larnaca stays mild while much of Europe is cold, which makes it a practical place to keep training through the winter. This page covers the conditions you can expect on the water and answers the questions sailors, parents and coaches ask us most often before booking a winter training camp.

Sailing conditions in Larnaca

Temperature

In November, daytime temperatures in Larnaca are around 20°C, so sailors can keep training outdoors well into the winter months.

Winter wind

In winter the wind can come from any direction and at any strength. That variety means sailors and coaches get to work on a wide range of conditions during a training block.

Summer wind

In the summer months the wind is a steady sea breeze from the south-southwest. It builds through the day and averages around 16 knots.

Frequently asked questions

What is the SailPro winter training base?

It is our training base at the Larnaca Nautical Club in Cyprus, set up for sailing teams, clubs and individual sailors who want to escape the cold and spend more time on the water. We provide Optimist and ILCA charter boats, coach RIBs, secure boat storage and a dedicated classroom for debriefs and theory sessions.

Which boats and equipment can we charter?

Our Optimist fleet is made up of 19 new, fully equipped Winner Optimist boats. ILCA sailors can choose between Devoti and Laser Ice Blue charter boats. We also have two fully equipped coach RIBs available for charter.

Can sailors join individually, or do we need to bring a team?

Both work. Sailors can join individually and train alongside the Larnaca Nautical Club team under the guidance of its coach, or come with their own coach and teammates to train together.

What facilities are available at the base?

Secure boat storage and a dedicated classroom for debriefs and theory sessions, along with the charter boats and coach RIBs described above.

What are the sailing conditions in winter?

Temperatures in November are around 20°C. The winter wind can come from any direction and at any strength, unlike the steady south-southwesterly sea breeze of summer, which averages around 16 knots.

When can we come for winter training?

We host sailors throughout the winter. Send us your preferred dates by email or WhatsApp and we will get back to you about availability.

How much does it cost and how do I book?

Contact us with your dates, the number of sailors and the boats and RIBs you need, and we will send you a quote. You can reach us by email at sailpro.cy@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +357 96774178.

Where is SailPro and how do I get there?

We are based at the Larnaca Nautical Club, Larnaca-Dhekelia Road, 6308, Larnaca, Cyprus. Larnaca International Airport serves the area.
