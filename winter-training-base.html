Winter Training Base

Leave the cold behind and keep training in the Mediterranean all winter long.
Our Larnaca training base is designed for sailing teams and clubs looking to escape the cold and maximise their time on the water. We provide fully equipped Optimist and ILCA charter boats, coach RIBs, secure boat storage, and a dedicated classroom for debriefs and theory sessions.
With warm weather and excellent sailing conditions throughout the winter, Larnaca is the perfect destination for productive off-season training. Whether you’re preparing for major regattas or maintaining momentum between events, SailPro provides the facilities, equipment, and support so sailors and coaches can focus entirely on performance.

Optimist Charter

Our fleet of 19 new, fully equipped Winner Optimist boats is available for charter to young sailors who want to leave the cold winter behind and continue progressing on the water.

ILCA Charter

ILCA sailors can choose between Devoti and Laser Ice Blue charter boats for their winter training in Cyprus.

RIB charter

Our fleet includes two fully equipped coach RIBs, available for charter to ensure coaches can deliver efficient and productive training sessions throughout the winter.
