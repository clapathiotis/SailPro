About Us

SailPro was born from a passion for sailing and a vision to create a year-round training base in Cyprus where sailors can grow, learn, and perform at their best. Founded in Larnaca, where the warm Mediterranean climate offers ideal sailing conditions throughout the year, SailPro provides professional coaching, top-quality charter boats, and tailored programs for ambitious sailors from around the world. Our goal is simple — to combine expert training with an inspiring environment that helps every sailor reach their full potential, both on and off the water.
